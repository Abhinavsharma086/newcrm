1. Employee Registration & User Role Management

Admin manages users under the **Employees** menu inside the sidebar.

How to Add a New Employee / User:

  1. Navigate to the **Employees** menu and click **Add Employee**.
  2. Fill in the employee's **Name**, **Email**, **Phone**, and secure login **Password**.
  3. Role Mapping: Assign one of the following roles from the dropdown:
    • Admin: Complete system access, system configuration changes, backup/restore, and approval overrides.
    • Business User: High-level manager. Evaluates dashboards, prints reports, and validates billing accounts. Bypasses core configurations.
    • Employee: Backoffice operations executive. Manages customer updates, tickets, and coordinates field worker logs.
    • Field Technician: Mobile-view access to LMC, pipeline measurements, and guided installation upload cards.
    • Field Marketing: Schedules initial visits, registers prospects, and maps leads.

2. CRM & Customer Administration

Customer entries can be created manually or uploaded in bulk.

Method A: Manual Addition

  1. Go to **Customers** and click **Add Customer**.
  2. Input core details: **Customer Name**, **Phone**, **Email**, **Society Name**, and **Street Address**.
  3. Auto-assignment: If a default contractor is mapped to the chosen society, the CRM will automatically route the customer to that contractor.

Method B: Excel Import

  1. Click the **Import Customers** button on the customer directory index.
  2. Upload your Excel sheet (the system supports standard columns mapping CRN No, BP ID, Address, Meter Serial, and LMC dates).

4. How the Employee & Technician Portal Works

Field workers access a simplified mobile-friendly dashboard built specifically for on-site operations.

A. Field Technician Flow

  1. Login: Technicians log in using their credentials mapped under the **Field Technician** role.
  2. LMC Stage Photos: When visiting a customer to perform LMC, the technician updates the customer profile by uploading 5 specific guided photos. The CRM shows sample images on screen for assistance:
    • Inside Kitchen Photo: Complete front view covering burner setup.
    • Meter Dial Photo: A close-up of the meter dial showing initial numbers clearly.
    • Outside Balcony Photo: External riser pipeline tapping segment.
    • RFC & JMR Reports: Uploads of signed forms.
  3. Appointment Audits & Videos: On the **Appointments** edit screen, technicians:
    • Select the **Burner Type** (Normal or Hob Stove).
    • Upload two short video clips: **Kitchen Setup Video** and **External Riser Pipe Video**.

B. Field Marketing Flow

  1. Lead Creation: Marketing executives register new societies, log customer interests, and record contact details on site.
  2. Scheduling visits: Marketing executives schedule visits that appear in the **Confirmed Appointments** directory for managers to assign.

C. Backoffice Operations Flow

  1. Reconciling Materials: Backoffice staff use the **FIM Reconciliation** panel to compare the number of meters/pipes checked out by a technician against what they actually installed at customer sites.
  2. Generating Invoices: Once the technician uploads JMR and LMC files, backoffice staff verify the details and generate GST-compliant invoices.
